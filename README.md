# 📊 FinMate – SmartBudget for Students

**Empowering Students to Master Their Finances**

FinMate (SmartBudget) is your financial ally – a personal finance assistant designed specifically for students. It simplifies expense tracking, shared cost management, savings insights, and even helps discover student discounts. Say goodbye to messy spreadsheets and hello to smarter money decisions!

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Expense Tracking**: Log and categorize expenses manually or automatically (with future bank integration).
- **Shared Cost Management**: Split costs easily with roommates or project teammates – no more awkward money talks.
- **Savings Insights**: Analyze spending habits and set saving goals with smart recommendations.
- **Bill Splitting**: Automatically calculate and track who owes what in group expenses.
- **Student Discounts**: Find and use exclusive deals to save more.

---

## 💡 The Problem We're Solving

Students often face:
- Limited and fluctuating income.
- Difficulty in tracking expenses across categories.
- Challenges in splitting bills fairly.
- Lack of financial literacy and savings planning.

FinMate aims to solve these with an intuitive and student-friendly digital solution.
